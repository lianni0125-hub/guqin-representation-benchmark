# Phonology Concept Boundary and Theory-Facing Implications

Date: 2026-10-16  
Status: proposed final terminology protocol for researcher approval

## 1. Three levels that must remain distinct

1. **Empirical object:** guqin notation, pitch, fingering/action and performed-event sequences.
2. **Borrowed theoretical principles:** contrast, feature factorization, contextual recoverability, tiered representation and representational economy.
3. **Claim type:** an external test of selected phonological principles, not prior identification of guqin as a natural-language phonological system.

The paper is a formal/computational phonology study because it tests the portability and limits of phonological representation principles. It is not entitled to call every musical regularity phonological.

## 2. Required positioning sentence

> This study does not presuppose that guqin music possesses a phonology equivalent to the phonology of natural language. Instead, it treats guqin notation–action sequences as an external test domain for selected principles of phonological representation, including contrast, feature factorization, contextual recoverability, and representational economy.

## 3. Terminology protocol

| Avoid as an unqualified result term | Use in this paper | Stronger evidence required before upgrading |
|---|---|---|
| guqin phoneme | event class; candidate structural contrast | linguistic function or independent contrast/alternation evidence |
| distinctive feature | action/pitch dimension; candidate contrastive feature | stable merge-loss across targets, environments and independent data |
| allophone | contextually predictable variant/class | linguistic distribution plus independent representational evidence |
| phonotactics | event-combination or sequential distribution | stable units and grammatical well-formedness evidence |
| underlying representation | analysis/model representation | behavioural, learning or cognitive evidence |
| cognitive underspecification | task-relative analytical non-specification | stable recoverability across relevant targets and environments |
| autonomous tier | separately encoded or tier-like factor | association/non-isomorphism evidence beyond improved regularization |
| guqin grammar | formal model of notation/action sequences | independently established generative competence and judgments |

`Phonological` may modify **theory, principle, method, comparison, or hypothesis**. It should not modify the guqin system as an established ontology unless the sentence explicitly says `candidate`, `phonology-informed`, or `phonological-style`.

## 4. Sign-language boundary

Sign-language phonology establishes that linguistic phonology is not restricted to the acoustic modality. It does not entail that every movement-based system has a phonology. Guqin actions must independently satisfy the registered contrast, generalization and economy tests; movement alone is not sufficient.

## 5. Theory-facing implications supported now

### TFI-1 — Factorization is more portable than phonological labels

The cross-domain value observed here lies in reusable feature factorization and contextual generalization, not in relabelling guqin events as phonemes. Atomic and high-cardinality flat codes generalize less economically than sparse action dimensions.

### TFI-2 — Structural complexity bears an empirical burden

Separate main effects can improve over flat conjunction, but explicit cross-tier association terms fail their cost/ablation test. A structure's formal availability or descriptive elegance does not establish representational necessity. Layers and links require independent generalizations or complexity-compensating fit.

### TFI-3 — Predictability is weaker than underspecification

Task-level recoverability varies by class and target: history improves broad motions yet fails rare return classes; one T2 action dimension is necessary while others are omissible or harmful. Statistical predictability therefore cannot by itself license deletion, allophony or cognitive underspecification. A stronger criterion requires stability across independent groups, relevant targets, ambiguity treatments and exceptions.

### TFI-4 — Economy is a Pareto relation

No unregistered scalar converts fit and complexity into a universal winner. The defensible result is a fit–coverage–cost frontier plus ablation evidence. This is a methodological proposal for evaluating competing explicit representations, not a claim that phonological grammars are logistic classifiers.

## 6. Theory-facing implications not supported

- guqin and language share the same cognitive grammar;
- action dimensions are universal phonological features;
- language phonology does not require association structures;
- a predictable category is absent from mental representation;
- R4-style tiers are cognitively real;
- the results establish a universal cross-domain representation law.

These stronger claims would require independent repertoire, another non-language domain and ideally a language dataset evaluated under the same fit–coverage–cost design, plus behavioural/perceptual evidence for cognitive claims.

## 7. Recommended disciplinary and title language

Preferred labels: `phonology-informed`, `formal-phonological principles`, `external test domain`, `beyond-language representation`, and `Super Linguistics`.

Avoid an unqualified title such as *The Phonology of Guqin*. Safer working-title forms include:

- *Testing Phonological Representation Beyond Language: Interpretable Representation Learning from Guqin Action Sequences*;
- *A Phonology-Informed Study of Representation and Predictability in Guqin Notation–Action Sequences*.
